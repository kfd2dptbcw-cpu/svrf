## 1. The numbers that set the budget

| | |
|---|---|
| List price | £9.99 |
| Royalty per copy (60% − print ≈ £2.80) | **≈ £3.20** |
| **Break-even ACOS** (ad spend ÷ ad sales) | **≈ 32%** (£3.20 ÷ £9.99) |
| Target ACOS while launching | up to 40%: a small loss is fine to get early reviews and ranking |
| Target ACOS once settled | under 25% |

When the How to Surf campaign settings come through, compare its bids and ACOS with these. A cheaper book has less room per click.

## 2. Amazon Ads: Sponsored Products (Amazon.co.uk)

Set up three campaigns so you can see what works. Start with **£3–£5 a day each** and run them for **2 weeks before changing anything**.

### Campaign A: Auto (discovery)
- Targeting: **Automatic**
- Default bid: **£0.30**
- Purpose: Amazon finds the search terms and product pages that convert. Harvest the winners into Campaign B.

### Campaign B: Manual keywords
Start bids at **£0.30–£0.40**. Use **Exact** match for the top 10 and **Phrase** match for the rest.

**Core (exact):**
surf journal · surf log book · surf logbook · surfing journal · surf diary · surf notebook · surfing log book · surf session log · gifts for surfers · surfer gifts

**Gift intent (phrase):**
surfing gifts for men · surfing gifts for women · surf gifts for him · surf gifts for her · gifts for surfers uk · surfer christmas gift · surf stocking filler · surfing birthday gift · gifts for surf lovers · surf dad gift

**Use intent (phrase):**
surf tracker · surf forecast book · swell log · wave log book · surfboard journal · surf trip journal · surf travel diary · surfing notebook for adults · cold water surfing · uk surfing book

**Learner crossover** (people buying beginner books are a good audience):
learn to surf book · how to surf book · surfing for beginners · surf lessons gift

### Campaign C: Product targeting
- **Your How to Surf paperback:** place the Surf Log on its product page. "Next step after you can stand up" is the natural pairing.
- **Competitor surf journals and log books** (from the Amazon.co.uk results for *surf journal*):
  - `B0F3JGL24L`
  - `B0GTRC9ZW9`
  - `1835236383` (ISBN-10)
  - `1908520396`: The Stormrider Surf Journal (ISBN-10)
  - Add more as you find them, up to 10–15. For a paperback with an ISBN, the ASIN is its **ISBN-10**.
- **Category:** *Surfing* (Sports & Outdoors › Water Sports › Surfing), refined to paperback and £5–£20.
- Bid: **£0.25–£0.35**

### Negative keywords (add to A and B)
kindle · ebook · free · audiobook · surf the web · internet · bodyboard · kids colouring · colouring book · wetsuit · surfboard wax · leash · fins

### Weekly routine (10 minutes)
1. Open the **Search term report** for Campaign A.
2. Any search term with **1 or more sales**: add it to Campaign B as **Exact** at the same bid, and as a **negative exact** in Campaign A, so the two campaigns don't compete.
3. Any keyword with **15+ clicks and no sale**: lower its bid by 30% or pause it.
4. Any keyword with ACOS **under 25%**: raise its bid by 10–15%.

### Cross-promotion with How to Surf
- Add a Campaign C-style product-targeting ad for **How to Surf** on the Surf Log page as well, so each book promotes the other.
- If the How to Surf listing allows A+ content, add a module along the lines of: "Learning? Log every session in the SVRF Surf Log." Use `marketing/04-aplus-look-inside.png`.

## 3. A+ content (KDP → Marketing → A+ Content)

Add the modules in this order:

**1. Standard Image Header With Text**: image `marketing/04-aplus-look-inside.png` (970 × 600)
- Headline: **Forecast first. Reality after.**
- Body: Write the forecast before you paddle out, log what you actually got, and give it a verdict. A tide curve to mark, a compass to draw swell and wind, and five circles to colour in: logging a session takes about a minute in the car park. A season later you'll know your spots, your boards and your forecasts better than any app.

**2. Standard Three Images & Text**
- Image 1 `marketing/06-aplus-square-session.png` · **120 session pages** · Forecast vs reality, tide, swell and wind compass, kit, crowd and stoke.
